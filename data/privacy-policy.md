# Privacy Policy for Vruta

**Last Updated:** May 11, 2026

Welcome to **Vruta**. We are dedicated to helping members of the Orthodox Jewish community find compatible _chavrutas_ (study partners). Because our service involves connecting individuals for religious study, we take your privacy and the security of your data very seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains how Vruta ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information in accordance with the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** and the **Data Protection Act 2018**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide our matching service, we collect the following personal information:

- **Identity & Profile Data:** Your name, gender, and profile picture.
- **Contact & Location Data:** The static address you provide. We do **not** track your live location via GPS.
- **Religious Affiliation:** Your synagogue (shul) membership. Under UK law, this is considered **Special Category Data** as it reveals religious beliefs.
- **Study Preferences:** What you are interested in learning and your weekly availability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

- **Matching Algorithm:** To suggest partners based on geographic proximity (using your static address), shared learning interests, and overlapping availability.
- **Identity Verification:** To maintain the safety and integrity of the Vruta community, we may contact the Rabbi or a designated administrator of the synagogue you have listed to confirm your identity and community membership.
- **Communication:** To notify you of potential matches or updates to the service.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

1.  **Consent:** By signing up and providing your synagogue details, you give us **explicit consent** to process your religious affiliation data for the purpose of finding a study partner.
2.  **Contract:** We process your profile and availability data to fulfill our service of matching you with others.
3.  **Legitimate Interests:** We verify identities with synagogue leaders to ensure the safety of our users and the trust of the community.

## 4. Sharing Your Data

- **With Other Users:** Potential matches will be able to see your name, profile picture, gender, learning interests, and general proximity. They will **not** see your exact street address.
- **With Synagogue Leadership:** We may share your name with your listed synagogue or Rabbi solely to verify that you are a known member of that community.
- **No Third-Party Sales:** We do not sell your personal data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

## 5. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical measures to keep your information secure.

- **Retention:** We keep your data only as long as you have an active account. If you choose to delete your account, your personal data will be removed from our active databases.
- **Storage:** Your data is stored on secure servers and is accessed only by authorized personnel for the purposes of matching and verification.

## 6. Your Rights

As a UK resident, you have the following rights regarding your data:

- **Access:** You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Correction:** You can ask us to update or correct inaccurate information.
- **Erasure:** You can request that we delete your data (the "Right to be Forgotten").
- **Withdrawal of Consent:** You can withdraw your consent for us to process your religious data at any time by closing your account.
